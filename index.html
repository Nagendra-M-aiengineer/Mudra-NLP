Abhinaya Darpana

Compiled by Acharya Nandikeshavara, the Abhinaya Darpana defines 28 Asamyuta Hastas (single hand gestures) and 24 Samyuta Hastas (double hand gestures), each with strict rules of formation and codified dramatic usages (Viniyogas).

Multilingual NLP Engine

The classification engine parses descriptive queries across 6 languages. Using multilingual tokenization, phonetic matching, and synonym weighting, it accurately predicts the intended classical gesture.
